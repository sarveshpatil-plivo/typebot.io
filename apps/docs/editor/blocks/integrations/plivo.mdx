title: Plivo
---

With the Plivo block, you can send an SMS message from your Plivo account during a conversation.

## Authentication

The block needs your Plivo `Auth ID` and `Auth Token`. You can find both on the [Plivo console](https://cx.plivo.com/dashboard/).

## Send SMS

This action sends an SMS message. It requires:

- `From` — a Plivo phone number in E.164 format (for example +14155551234), a short code, or an approved sender ID.
- `To` — the recipient phone number in E.164 format.
- `Message` — the text to send.

You can optionally save the returned message UUID into a variable.
